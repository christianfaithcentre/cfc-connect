Who Is God?

God is the creator of everything — and He created you on purpose, with purpose. He's not distant or angry. He's a loving Father who knows you by name and wants a real relationship with you.

What Is the Gospel?

The word "Gospel" simply means good news. Here's the short version: we were separated from God, but He loved us so much that He sent His Son, Jesus, to bridge that gap. Jesus lived a perfect life, died on the cross to take the penalty for our mistakes, and rose again three days later. Because of that, anyone who believes in Him can have a fresh start — forgiven, loved, and never alone.

What Does It Mean to Follow Jesus?

It means inviting Him into your life — not because you have it all together, but because you're ready to stop doing it alone. Following Jesus is a relationship, not a religion. It's a daily choice to trust Him, grow with His people, and become who He made you to be.

Ready to Take That Step?

If you'd like to give your life to Jesus today, you can pray this simple prayer:

"Jesus, I believe You are the Son of God. I believe You died for me and rose again. I'm sorry for the ways I've fallen short. I invite You into my life. Be my Lord and my Savior. Thank You for loving me. Amen."

If you just prayed that prayer — welcome to the family. We'd love to walk with you. Tap Take Your Next Step on the home screen to let us know.
